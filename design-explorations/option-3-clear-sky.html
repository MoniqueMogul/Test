How it works

One story arc across the whole trip

The field trip stops being an island. FarmQuest connects it to the days before and after, so the learning compounds.

Before · in class

Launch the story on Chromebooks

Students join with a group code, meet the characters, and make opening choices. The farm chapters quietly download to their phones for offline use.

During · at the farm

Scan stations, unlock chapters

Permanent QR signs at the barn, field, and market stand open location-specific chapters. Choices reference real things students can see and check.

After · back in class

Finish with a branching finale

Each group's ending reflects their choices. A built-in reflection chapter and CSV export turn the trip into assessable learning.

Built for teachers

You approve every word before students play

AI drafts a branching story pack customized to your farm, grade band, and subject focus — overnight. You review the full story tree, regenerate any section in one click, and approve. Until you do, students see nothing.
