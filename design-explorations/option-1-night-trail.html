One quest. Three worlds.

A single adventure arcs across the classroom, the farm, and back — so the trip is a chapter, not a day off.

1
In class · Chromebooks

The Setup

Students meet the characters, take on roles, and make their first choices. A mystery is planted — and the on-site chapters quietly cache to their phones.

2
At the farm · Their phones

The Expedition

QR codes at real stations unlock chapters tied to what students are standing in front of. Clues live in the real world: feed charts, soil beds, equipment tags.

3
Back in class · Reflection

The Reckoning

The finale resolves differently for every group, based on their accumulated choices. A story-map recap shows the path they carved — and what it taught them.

Built for the people behind the players

Game energy for students. Zero-drama logistics for everyone else.

🛡️

Teacher-approved, always

AI drafts the branching story pack overnight; teachers read every branch and approve before a single student can play. No unreviewed text, ever.

📵

Offline where the cows are

Chapters cache during the pre-visit lesson, so dead zones don't kill the quest. Choices sync when signal returns.

🎓

Curriculum in costume

Ag science, food systems, sustainability, and careers — woven into the plot, matched to grade band 6–8 or 9–12.

🚜

Farms do it once

A 30-minute onboarding and a set of weatherproof QR signs. After that, every class trip runs itself.
