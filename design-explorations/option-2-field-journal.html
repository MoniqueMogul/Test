Every farm has a story. Your class gets to live one.

FarmQuest is an interactive story for grades 6–12 that begins in the classroom, unfolds chapter by chapter at a real local farm, and ends back at school — shaped entirely by the choices your students make along the way.

how the journey goes

Three chapters, three places

📖
Chapter One · The Classroom

Something's wrong at Hilltop Farm

On Chromebooks, students meet the farm, pick their roles, and make the first choices of the mystery. The trip ahead becomes a story they're already inside.

🚜
Chapter Two · The Farm

The clues are real

At the barn, the pasture, the market stand — QR signs unlock the next pages. The story asks students to look up from the screen: the answers are standing right in front of them.

🌾
Chapter Three · Back at School

Every group ends differently

The finale resolves by the choices each group made. A reflection chapter ties the tale to what they saw, touched, and learned at the farm.

for teachers & parents

Old-fashioned promises

✓Read before it's played

Stories are drafted with AI, then read and approved by the teacher — every branch, every page — before any student sees a word.

✓No student accounts

Students join with a simple group code. No names, no emails, no data trail. Parents can read every page their child read.

✓Works without signal

The farm chapters are tucked into students' phones before the trip, so the story never depends on a bar of reception.

✓Curriculum underneath

Agriculture science, food systems, sustainability, and rural careers — folded into the plot, matched to the grade level you teach.
