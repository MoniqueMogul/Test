# EQIP Application-Readiness Checklist

**Program:** Environmental Quality Incentives Program (EQIP), USDA Natural Resources Conservation Service (NRCS)
**Reference page:** [EQIP Advance Payment Option](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/resources/guides-and-instructions/eqip-advance-payment-option)

> **Important framing:** EQIP is not a traditional "grant." It is a **cost-share / financial assistance contract** — NRCS pays you a set rate for implementing approved conservation practices on land you farm, ranch, or manage as forest. You are not "awarded a grant"; you are **selected for a contract** through a competitive ranking process. The page you referenced covers the **Advance Payment Option**, an extra benefit that lets **historically underserved producers** receive at least 50% of a practice payment *up front* instead of being reimbursed after the work is done.

---

## 1. Basic Eligibility — Confirm You Qualify

- [ ] You are an **agricultural producer, forest landowner, or Indian Tribe** engaged in livestock, crop, or forest production (or have an interest in an agricultural/forestry operation).
- [ ] You **own or control the land** for the full term of the proposed contract (typically 1–10 years). Renters/lessees qualify with documented control.
- [ ] The land is **eligible land**: cropland, rangeland, pastureland, non-industrial private forestland, or other farm/ranch land.
- [ ] Your average **Adjusted Gross Income (AGI) is at or below $900,000** per year (averaged over the 3 preceding tax years), unless an exemption applies.
- [ ] You are in compliance with **Highly Erodible Land and Wetland Conservation** provisions (form AD-1026 on file with FSA).
- [ ] You have at least one **natural resource concern** on your land that EQIP practices can address (e.g., soil erosion, water quality, irrigation efficiency, grazing land health, wildlife habitat, air quality).

## 2. Establish Your USDA Farm Records (do this FIRST — it gates everything)

Visit your local **USDA Service Center** (find it at [farmers.gov/service-center-locator](https://www.farmers.gov/working-with-us/service-center-locator)) and work with the **Farm Service Agency (FSA)** to:

- [ ] Establish (or update) your **farm record** and obtain a **farm number and tract number** for the land.
- [ ] Register your **official Tax ID** (Social Security Number or Employer ID Number).
- [ ] If operating as an entity (LLC, partnership, trust), have your **entity paperwork and signature authority** documented, and obtain a **DUNS/UEI registration in SAM.gov** if required for entities.
- [ ] File **Form AD-1026** (Highly Erodible Land Conservation and Wetland Conservation Certification).
- [ ] File **Form CCC-941** (Adjusted Gross Income certification, covering the prior 3 tax years).
- [ ] File **Form CCC-902** (Farm Operating Plan) so FSA can determine payment eligibility.
- [ ] Set up **direct deposit** (SF-3881 / banking info) for program payments.
- [ ] Create a **farmers.gov account** (login.gov) so you can view/manage applications, conservation plans, and maps online.

## 3. Gather Your Documents

- [ ] **Tax ID** — Social Security card or IRS documentation of EIN.
- [ ] **Proof of land control** — property deed, or a written **lease agreement** covering the full contract term.
- [ ] If you lease: **written authorization from the landowner** to install structural or vegetative practices.
- [ ] **Farm and tract numbers** (from Step 2).
- [ ] **AGI figures** from your last 3 tax returns (needed for CCC-941).
- [ ] **Maps/boundaries** of the fields or areas where you want conservation work (NRCS can help produce these).
- [ ] If applicable: **organic certification** or proof of transition to organic (for the EQIP Organic Initiative).
- [ ] If applicable: **irrigation history documentation** (required for irrigation-related practices in many states).
- [ ] If operating as an entity: articles of organization/incorporation, member lists, and signature authority documents.

## 4. Determine Historically Underserved (HU) Status — Unlocks the Advance Payment Option + Higher Payment Rates

Check whether you qualify in **any** of these categories (self-certified on your application):

- [ ] **Beginning Farmer or Rancher** — you have operated a farm/ranch for **less than 10 consecutive years**.
- [ ] **Veteran Farmer or Rancher** — served in the armed forces AND (new to farming, farmed < 10 years, or first obtained veteran status within the last 10 years).
- [ ] **Socially Disadvantaged Farmer or Rancher** — member of a group subjected to racial or ethnic prejudice.
- [ ] **Limited Resource Farmer or Rancher** — household income at or below the national poverty level / meets the limited-resource test at the [LRF/R Self-Determination Tool](https://lrftool.sc.egov.usda.gov/).

**If you qualify as HU, you get:**
- [ ] Eligibility for the **Advance Payment Option** — at least **50% of each practice payment paid up front** to purchase materials or contract services.
- [ ] **Higher EQIP payment rates** (up to 90% cost-share vs. the standard ~75%).
- [ ] Dedicated HU funding pools in many states (you compete against other HU applicants, not the general pool).

**Advance Payment rules to plan for:**
- [ ] You elect advance payments when your contract/plan of operations (form **NRCS-CPA-1155**) is set up — tell NRCS you want it.
- [ ] Advance funds **must be spent within 90 days of receipt**, and the practice must be completed as agreed in your EQIP plan of operations — line up your vendors/contractors *before* requesting the advance.
- [ ] Alternative: choose **vendor direct deposit**, where NRCS pays your vendor or contractor directly (no 90-day clock on you).

## 5. Work With NRCS Before You Apply

- [ ] **Contact your local NRCS District Conservationist** — introduce yourself and describe your operation and goals.
- [ ] Schedule a **site visit / one-on-one consultation** so the conservation planner can assess resource concerns on your land.
- [ ] Develop a **conservation plan / EQIP plan of operations** with NRCS that addresses at least one natural resource concern and lists the specific **conservation practices** you'll implement (e.g., cover crops, fencing, irrigation upgrades, high tunnels, waste storage, brush management).
- [ ] Ask which **funding pools, state initiatives, and ranking pools** fit your operation (organic, high tunnel, wildlife, drought, HU pools, etc.).
- [ ] Ask about **payment schedules/rates** for the practices you want, so you know what NRCS will pay vs. your out-of-pocket share.

## 6. Submit the Application

- [ ] Complete **Form NRCS-CPA-1200** (Conservation Program Application) — your NRCS conservationist will complete and submit it with you. ([Form + appendix PDF](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2023-11/EQIP%20Application%20Form%20and%20Appendix.pdf))
- [ ] Applications are accepted **year-round**, but funding is decided in **state-specific ranking/batching periods** — ask your local office for your **state's ranking deadline** and submit before it.
- [ ] Confirm NRCS has verified **both you and your land** as eligible (this uses the FSA records from Step 2 — incomplete FSA records are the #1 reason applications stall).

## 7. Ranking, Selection, and Contract

- [ ] Your application is **ranked competitively** against others based on local resource concerns, expected conservation benefit, cost-effectiveness, and applicant needs.
- [ ] If selected, **review and sign the EQIP contract** (you may decline with no penalty).
- [ ] Do **not start any practice before the contract is signed** — work begun early is generally ineligible for payment.
- [ ] If you're HU and want advance payments, **elect the Advance Payment Option** at contract signing (NRCS-CPA-1155) and request funds when ready to buy materials or hire contractors.
- [ ] Implement practices to **NRCS standards and specifications**, on the schedule in your plan of operations.
- [ ] After completion (or after receiving an advance), submit documentation/certification so NRCS can verify the practice and issue payment.
- [ ] If not selected: your application typically **rolls to the next ranking period** — ask how to improve your ranking score.

## Quick-Start Action Plan (in order)

1. **This week:** Locate your USDA Service Center; call to schedule appointments with both FSA (farm records) and NRCS (conservation consultation).
2. **FSA visit:** Get your farm number; file AD-1026, CCC-941, CCC-902; set up direct deposit and farmers.gov access.
3. **NRCS visit:** Site assessment, discuss resource concerns, identify practices, and self-certify HU status if applicable.
4. **Ask for your state's next EQIP ranking deadline** and target it.
5. **Submit CPA-1200** with your conservationist before the deadline.
6. **At contract signing:** elect the Advance Payment Option (if HU) or vendor direct deposit.
